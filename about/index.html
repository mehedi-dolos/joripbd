About the firm

An independent firm, in practice for over thirty years.

Jorip O Paridarshan Company Limited is a private limited company of surveyors, valuers and loss assessors based in Purana Paltan, Dhaka. Banks, financial institutions and insurance companies appoint us to value property, verify stock and assess insured losses.

Independence

We report the value we find.

Our task is to arrive at the present value of a property, or the true extent of a loss, without bias toward the party that appointed us or the party being assessed. We carry out every valuation, assessment and adjustment independently and without influence.

That is the whole reason an institution appoints an outside surveyor, and it is the standard we hold ourselves to. It is also the firm's slogan, unchanged since it was founded: we vouch for professionalism and honesty.

In memoriam

Ataur Rahman, founder and Chief Surveyor

Mr. Ataur Rahman served as Dhaka Office Manager of SGS (Geneva) from 1966 to 1975, where he was trained in quality and quantity inspection and later as an assessor, valuer and insurance loss adjuster. He founded JOP, led it as Managing Director and Chief Surveyor, and personally trained every surveyor and inspector the firm employed.

Bangladesh knew him as Monchosharothi, the charioteer of the stage: a founder of Nagorik Natya Sampradaya, director of more than thirty-five plays, and a recipient of the Ekushey Padak and the Independence Award. The same exactness he brought to the theatre he brought to every survey report that carried his signature.

The firm today

Thirty people, one office, the whole country.

Thirty technical and administrative staff work from the corporate office at Paltan Tower. Field surveyors cover assignments across Bangladesh, from Cox's Bazar to Sreemangal, while the office handles documentation, report preparation and liaison with the appointing institution.
