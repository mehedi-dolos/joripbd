steps:
  - title: Appointment
    text: The insurer appoints us on notice of loss, with the policy schedule and the insured's claim intimation.
  - title: Site visit
    text: Our surveyor attends the site, records the damage, interviews the insured and secures salvage.
  - title: Investigation
    text: We establish cause and extent, collect invoices, stock records and repair estimates, and verify them.
  - title: Quantification
    text: The loss is quantified at the value the policy contemplates, with salvage and recoverables noted.
  - title: Report
    text: A final survey report to the insurer, with a copy of the findings the insured has seen.

When a loss occurs the insurer needs an independent account of what happened and what it cost. We attend quickly, document carefully and quantify conservatively, so that both the insurer and the insured can rely on the figure.
