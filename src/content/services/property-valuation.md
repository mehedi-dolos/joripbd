Property valuation is the service most of our bank clients appoint us for. A lender needs a figure it can rely on before sanction, at periodic review and again at recovery. We produce that figure from a physical inspection by our own surveyor, verified against the documents, and we state the basis so the credit committee can see how it was reached.
