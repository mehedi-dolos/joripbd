  - title: Physical count

  - title: Reconciliation
    text: We reconcile the physical findings against the declaration and note every discrepancy.
  - title: Report
    text: A survey report with quantities, condition, valuation as instructed, and photographs.

    text: We answer queries from the appointing institution until the file closes.
