turnaround: Adjustment report within ten working days of receiving the insured's final documents.
steps:
  - title: Assessment received
    text: The adjustment starts from the survey findings, whether our own or another surveyor's.
  - title: Policy review
    text: We check that the loss falls within cover and note every term that affects the amount payable.
  - title: Computation
    text: Excess, average, depreciation and salvage are applied and the net payable is computed.
  - title: Agreement
    text: We discuss the figure with the insured and record any agreement or dispute.
  - title: Report
    text: An adjustment report to the insurer with the recommended settlement.
---
Assessment tells the insurer what was lost. Adjustment tells the insurer what the policy pays. We keep the two distinct, and we set out every step from the assessed loss to the net payable so the settlement can be checked by anyone who reads the report.
