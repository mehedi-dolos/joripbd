Services

What we are appointed to do.

Four service lines, each delivered by our own surveyors and signed off in the Paltan office. Choose a service to see what it covers, what you receive and how to appoint us.

  1. 01 / 04

    Property Valuation

    A defensible market value and forced-sale value for any property a bank is asked to lend against.

    Full details

    Covers

    • Land, residential and commercial buildings
    • Factories, warehouses and industrial sheds
    • Plant, machinery and equipment
    • Vehicles, vessels and other movable assets

    Appointed by

    • Banks and non-bank financial institutions (credit, recovery and legal departments)
    • Corporates and developers
    • Individual owners who need a report a bank will accept
  2. 02 / 04

    Surveying & Inspection

    Independent confirmation that the stock, goods or work claimed on paper exist, in the quantity and condition stated.

    Full details

    Covers

    • Verification of hypothecated and pledged stock for banks
    • Inspection of stores, raw materials and finished goods
    • Pre-shipment and post-landing inspection of quantity and quality
    • Tally, sampling and condition surveys

    Appointed by

    • Banks and non-bank financial institutions
    • Importers, exporters and trading houses
    • Insurance companies
    • Project lenders and sponsors
  3. 03 / 04

    Loss Assessment

    The cause, extent and quantum of an insured loss, established on site and documented so the claim can move.

    Full details

    Covers

    • Fire and allied perils
    • Marine cargo and inland transit
    • Engineering: machinery breakdown, erection all risks, contractors' all risks
    • Burglary, money and miscellaneous classes

    Appointed by

    • Insurance companies
    • The insured, where the policy allows an independent assessment
  4. 04 / 04

    Insurance Adjustment

    The assessed loss applied to the policy, and a settlement figure both sides can accept.

    Full details

    Covers

    • Verification of coverage, sums insured, warranties and conditions
    • Application of excess, average (under-insurance) and depreciation
    • Salvage valuation and disposal
    • Negotiation with the insured on quantum

    Appointed by

    • Insurance companies, under IDRA licence

How an assignment runs

The same sequence, whatever the service.

  1. Step 01

    Appointment

    The bank, insurer or client appoints us in writing.

    Appointment letter
  2. Step 02

    Site inspection

    Our surveyor visits with the borrower, insured or branch.

    Field notes and photographs
  3. Step 03

    Verification

    Deeds, mutation, approved plans, invoices and market enquiry.

    Verified file
  4. Step 04

    Report

    A signed report with the figure, its basis, photographs and site plan.

    Signed report
  5. Step 05

    Clarification

    We answer queries from the appointing institution until the file closes.

    File closed
