Insurance Adjustment

The assessed loss applied to the policy, and a settlement figure both sides can accept.

Assessment tells the insurer what was lost. Adjustment tells the insurer what the policy pays. We keep the two distinct, and we set out every step from the assessed loss to the net payable so the settlement can be checked by anyone who reads the report.

What it covers

  • Verification of coverage, sums insured, warranties and conditions
  • Application of excess, average (under-insurance) and depreciation
  • Salvage valuation and disposal
  • Negotiation with the insured on quantum
  • Settlement recommendation to the insurer

What you receive

  • An adjustment report with the recommended net payable amount
  • The computation from assessed loss to net payable, line by line
  • The policy provisions relied on and the documents examined

How it runs

  1. Step 01

    Assessment received

    The adjustment starts from the survey findings, whether our own or another surveyor's.

  2. Step 02

    Policy review

    We check that the loss falls within cover and note every term that affects the amount payable.

  3. Step 03

    Computation

    Excess, average, depreciation and salvage are applied and the net payable is computed.

  4. Step 04

    Agreement

    We discuss the figure with the insured and record any agreement or dispute.

  5. Step 05

    Report

    An adjustment report to the insurer with the recommended settlement.

Turnaround. Adjustment report within ten working days of receiving the insured's final documents.
