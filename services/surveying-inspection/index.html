Surveying & Inspection

Independent confirmation that the stock, goods or work claimed on paper exist, in the quantity and condition stated.

Inspection is where the firm began. Our founder managed the Dhaka office of SGS, the world’s largest inspection company, and the same discipline of counting, weighing, sampling and recording applies whether the assignment is a bank’s stock verification or a shipment at the port.

What it covers

  • Verification of hypothecated and pledged stock for banks
  • Inspection of stores, raw materials and finished goods
  • Pre-shipment and post-landing inspection of quantity and quality
  • Tally, sampling and condition surveys
  • Progress monitoring of construction and machinery installation
  • Identification and marking of assets under charge

What you receive

  • A survey report with quantities counted, weighed or measured
  • Condition and quality findings against the declared specification
  • Valuation at cost or market as instructed
  • Photographs and discrepancies against the declaration

How it runs

  1. Step 01

    Appointment

    The bank or trader appoints us with the stock statement, invoices or shipping documents to be verified.

  2. Step 02

    Physical count

    Our inspector counts, weighs or measures on site, samples where the goods allow, and records condition.

  3. Step 03

    Reconciliation

    We reconcile the physical findings against the declaration and note every discrepancy.

  4. Step 04

    Report

    A survey report with quantities, condition, valuation as instructed, and photographs.

  5. Step 05

    Clarification

    We answer queries from the appointing institution until the file closes.

Turnaround. Stock verification inside Dhaka within two working days of appointment. Port and factory inspections are scheduled to the shipment or production date.
