Team

Thirty people, trained in one method.

Surveyors and inspectors in the field, and an office team that turns their measurements into a report an institution can rely on.

How the team works

One method, from the founder down.

01

Trained in-house

Every surveyor learned the firm’s method on the job, first from the founder and now from the senior surveyors.

02

Never alone on a first visit

A new surveyor accompanies a senior colleague until the senior signs off on their measurements and photographs.

03

Countrywide

Field surveyors travel from Dhaka to any district. The office prepares the report while the surveyor is still on site.
